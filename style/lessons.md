# Lessons

Dated rules distilled from the Author's edits and notes. Each entry: an id, a date and a
category in the heading; `Rule`, `Provenance`, `Weight`, `Language` lines; optional `Before`
and `After` quotes taken from the actual edit. Nothing lands here without the Author's
confirmation. Lessons record what the Author said, never a model's reading of the Author.

## lesson-0001 · 2026-10-22 · dialogue

Rule: A character must not voice the author's thesis or spell out the parallel; the reader assembles it alone.
Provenance: Author's note on «Черепа и Кости: часть 1 — Старые Друзья» (Appendix A.1 of the build prompt).
Weight: 3
Language: both

Before:
> «Правильно. Ты бы не успел. Я считал. Я тоже считаю.»

After:
> The two of them count in silence; neither says what the numbers mean.

## lesson-0002 · 2026-10-22 · labeling

Rule: No label tags with name, age and place («Рубио. 36. Австралия»), least of all at the strongest point of a chapter. An inscription, a card or a formula is shown once; afterwards only as a detail.
Provenance: Author's note on «Черепа и Кости: часть 1 — Старые Друзья» (Appendix A.1 of the build prompt).
Weight: 3
Language: both

Before:
> Рубио. 36. Австралия.

After:
> The card is shown once, in someone's hand, and never listed again.
