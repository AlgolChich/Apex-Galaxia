# Voice card

## Sentence rhythm

- Short declaratives for action, one long run-on sentence of counting per chapter.
- Repetition with a turn: "once as a passenger and once as a problem."

## Paragraph habits

- Two to five sentences; dialogue lines stand alone.
- Paragraphs open on an object or an action, never on a feeling.

## Dialogue attribution

- "said" or nothing; attribution after the first line only.

## Favorite constructions

- Counting as a way to show state of mind.
- A rule painted on a door, quoted once, then only glanced at.

## Register

- Plain, concrete; working nouns (punch, urn, thermos, line) carry the mood.

## Never uses

- Named emotions; rhetorical questions in narration; exclamation marks.
