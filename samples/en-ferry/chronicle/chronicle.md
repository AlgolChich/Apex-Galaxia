# Chronicle

_One entry after each chapter: what changed, what is ripening, what is open. Only what has already happened._

## Chapter 1

The last ferry of the season has eleven passengers and ten tickets. The eleventh is an old woman with a cardboard suitcase and a ticket dated for a crossing that does not exist. Halloran wants her off the boat; June has seen the name on her luggage tag before, on the brass plate for the lost crewman.
