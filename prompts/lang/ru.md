Language module: Russian.

- Write in living literary Russian: no calques from English, no officialese, no translated syntax (possessive pronouns where Russian drops them, chains of participles, stacked passives, «является», «данный», «осуществлять»).
- Dialogue is set with em dashes, one speaker per paragraph: «— Реплика, — сказал он. — Продолжение реплики.» No quotation marks around speech. A line that continues after attribution starts with a lowercase letter after the comma-dash.
- Quotation marks for titles and quoted words are «ёлочки»; nested quotes are „лапки“.
- The letter ё: {{yo_policy}}. Never mix the two policies in one text.
- Attribution is «сказал», «сказала» or nothing; not a gesture on every line; no said-bookisms («прошипел он с ухмылкой»).
- Names of fandom characters come from the official Russian localization as written in the glossary; never transliterate ad hoc.
- Vary sentence length; short paragraphs for phones. Avoid the clichés in the banned list, for example: {{banned_examples}}.
- Swearing only when in character and within the rating.
- Numbers under one hundred in words inside prose; dates and times as the characters would say them.
