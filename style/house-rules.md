# House rules

These rules are binding in both languages. They seed every prose prompt, drive the critic
rubric and the linter, and are checked before a chapter is shown. They were assembled from the
Author's working memo (written over «Черепа и Кости: часть 1 — Старые Друзья», living authors
on AO3, Chekhov, and the official Russian lines of Team Fortress 2 characters). Where the
Author's Russian original (`docs/seed/style-memo.ru.md`) differs, the Russian text wins.

## Prose

- Show, don't tell: a concrete detail instead of a general word, a strong verb instead of an adverb. Feeling comes through hands, pauses, objects and what is left unsaid; name an emotion only where it cannot be shown.
- Rhythm: short sentences for tension, long ones for breath. Vary paragraph length. Consecutive paragraphs never open with the same word. Short and medium paragraphs: stories are read on phones.
- Exposition through state, not past: what is left of whom, not what happened to whom. Facts surface in an argument, a deal or an evasion. «As you know» is forbidden.
- Every chapter has one scene that decides nothing: food, repair, silence, daily life.
- The antagonist has one bell-phrase; after its first use it frightens on its own and nobody explains it.
- A chapter ends on an image, not a conclusion. No moral, no «what we learned», no narrator intrusion, no meta-commentary inside the text.
- First appearance of a character: a portrait woven into action, not a form; 3–5 precise details and one unexpected; afterwards only strokes. Wounds, dirt and changes of clothing are recorded in the chronicle.
- Characters act from their own logic, not from the plot's needs. If the plot needs an out-of-character act, build the circumstances that make it logical for that character.
- Mistakes come from character, missing information or a clash of values, never from stupidity. The antagonist is clever, answers the heroes' moves, and does not wait for them.
- Coincidence may create a problem, never solve one. Every gun is planted before it fires.
- Ground each scene in two or three senses: sound, smell, texture, temperature, light. Never all of them.
- By the end of the story the hero visibly changes or visibly fails to change.

## Dialogue

1. Nobody states the theme. A line that sounds like the conclusion of the scene is cut.
2. Every line is a move: the speaker wants something, hides something or dodges something.
3. Answers miss: people answer the wrong question, interrupt, stay silent, change the subject.
4. In a quarrel everyone is unfair and hits beside the mark; people argue from their wound, not as each other's lawyers (Chekhov, «Враги»: the unhappy are selfish and do not hear each other).
5. Subtext is made by writing the honest line first, then forbidding the character its key word, the feeling or the request.
6. Attribution: «said» («сказал») or nothing; not a gesture on every line.
7. Roughness is allowed: a cut-off, a repeat, an awkward joke, talk about soup. The same phrase said twice with different weight beats a new one.
8. A character in a scene has a small immediate want: a cigarette, water, to be left alone. This brings a scene alive faster than any monologue.
9. Hide the name: it must still be clear who is speaking. A character's vocabulary comes from official localization pages (for fandoms) or the voice block, never from «a general impression». A killer or a madman justifies himself in the language of his trade, not as a psychiatrist. A voice may fall apart from pain or fatigue, but each one differently: one shrinks to two words, another slides into gibberish.

## AI marks the Author removes

- Negative parallelism: «не X, а Y», «не потому что… а потому что», «Не нож. Ожидание», «not X but Y», «it's not just X, it's Y».
- Triads: «первое… второе… третье», three adjectives in a row, symmetrical lists.
- The summarizing sentence after a scene («это было самое страшное, что он видел») and the comparative wrap-up («и это было хуже, чем…»).
- Chopped aphoristic fragments instead of action.
- Equal paragraph lengths; a device, inscription or formula repeated more than once.
- A character who narrates his own psychology; a character voicing the author's thesis.
- Label tags with name, age and place («Рубио. 36. Австралия») at the strongest point of a chapter.
- Russian and English clichés from the banned lists (`banned-phrases.ru.txt`, `banned-phrases.en.txt`); em-dash chains, said-bookisms, translated syntax in Russian (calques, officialese).
- Structural tells: the deus ex machina, the dream reveal, a moral at the end.

## Checklist before a chapter is shown

- A line that sounds like the scene's conclusion? Cut.
- «не X, а Y», triads, summary sentences? Rewrite.
- Cover the names: are the voices distinguishable?
- Weighed, fair arguments in a quarrel? Break them.
- Any template repeated more than once? Keep one.
- Paragraphs of different length? A breather scene present?
- Labels with age and place? Remove.
