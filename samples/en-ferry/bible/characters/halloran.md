# Captain Halloran

## Summary

Master of the ferry for thirty years; wants the last run over with, and will not say why.

## True now

In the wheelhouse with a thermos he has not opened. Watching the strait more than the passengers.

## History

Lost a crewman in the strait eleven years ago, the week before the freeze. Has never run this late in the season since.

## Appearance

Heavy, stooped, a wool cap worn through at the crown; hands that shake until they hold the wheel; a wedding ring on a chain, not a finger.

## Voice

Half sentences. "Get her off." "Not tonight." Never finishes an apology. Bell-phrase: "We don't stop in the strait."

## Wants, needs, obstacle

Wants to reach the mainland before dark. Needs to forgive himself for the crewman. The old woman makes him stop in the strait.

## Relationships

June: trusts her count, not her questions.

## Secrets

He recognizes the old woman. She is the crewman's mother.
