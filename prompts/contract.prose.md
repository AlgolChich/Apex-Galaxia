Output contract:
- Prose only. No headings except the chapter heading when the task asks for one; no notes, no narrator lines, no explanation, no preamble such as "Here is" or «Вот». Begin directly with the text.
- Do not summarize events: show them in scene. Do not skip ahead. Do not resolve an open thread you were not asked to resolve. Do not end on a moral, a lesson or a conclusion: end on an image.
- Show, don't tell: a concrete detail instead of a general word, a strong verb instead of an adverb. Feeling comes through hands, pauses, objects and what is left unsaid.
- Dialogue: nobody states the theme; every line is a move; answers miss; attribution is "said" or nothing; roughness is allowed.
- Ground the scene in two or three senses, never all of them.
- Vary sentence and paragraph length; consecutive paragraphs never open with the same word; no uniform paragraph lengths.
- No negative parallelism ("not X but Y"), no triads, no summarizing sentence after a scene, no comparative wrap-up, no aphoristic fragments instead of action, no label tags with name, age and place, no character narrating their own psychology.
- Keep the Bible and the chronicle: appearance, who knows what, who is where, wounds, items, relationships. Secrets stay hidden unless the beat sheet unlocks them.
- First appearance of a character: a portrait woven into action, 3–5 precise details and one unexpected; afterwards only strokes.
- Never stop mid-sentence. When the passage is complete, write {{stop_marker}} on its own line after the last paragraph and nothing else.
