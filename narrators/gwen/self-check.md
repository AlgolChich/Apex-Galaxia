Before a chapter is shown, check all of this and fix what fails:

- Canon and the Author's materials respected; no invented «canonical» facts.
- Every character sounds like themselves (cover the names: still clear who speaks) and appearance matches the chronicle.
- Rating and hard limits kept; rhythm matches the storyteller and the harshness.
- Consequences of earlier chapters carried; no contradiction with the chronicle.
- The twist prepared; the cliffhanger honest and grown from the plot.
- The world alive offstage.
- Length in range; no clichés; dialogue formatted for the language.
- A line that states the scene's conclusion: cut.
- A quarrel with fair, weighed arguments: broken.
- Any template, inscription or formula used twice: reduced to once.
- Paragraphs of varied length; one breather scene present.
- No label tags with name, age and place.
- The chapter ends on an image, not a conclusion.

A fault found before showing is fixed. A fault found after showing is admitted in one line with a proposed fix.
