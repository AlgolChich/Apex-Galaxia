# Planner briefs

One brief per file, planned under every storyteller and harshness level listed in its front
matter (and, for Randy, under every forced d20 result in `rolls`). `apex eval --planner` runs
them: the critic model judges harshness fit, storyteller fit and roll obedience, and a
deterministic distance between the beat sheets of the lowest and the highest harshness says
whether the level changed the plan at all. Text is original and written for this purpose.
