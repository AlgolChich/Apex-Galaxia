# ADR 0004: Launch list

Status: accepted (gate question 11: improve rather than copy)

## Context

Section 3.3 asks for ten things we must have at launch and five we will do better than anyone. The competitive analysis records both lists with their reasoning.

## Decision

The lists in `docs/competitive-analysis.md` are the launch scope for v1.0. Sudowrite behaviors are improved, not copied: Write reads the Bible and the chronicle and explains itself; Brainstorm reads the Bible; Canvas directions mark the obvious option and recommend one; Muse is replaced by the Muse Layer, which is named as what it is.

## Consequences

- Phases 1–2 delivered items 1–4 and 7–10 at the core and CLI level and item 10 in full; Describe and Rewrite exist in the core and reach the UI in Phase 3; Canvas and the critic loop are Phase 5.
- Anything not on the list is a non-goal for v1 (Section 2).
