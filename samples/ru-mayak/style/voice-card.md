# Голосовая карточка

## Sentence rhythm

- Short declarative sentences for action; one long sentence per paragraph at most.
- Repetition of a concrete phrase with different weight («Катера не было видно»).

## Paragraph habits

- Three to five sentences; dialogue lines stand alone.
- Paragraphs open on an object or an action, never on a feeling.

## Dialogue attribution

- «сказал» or nothing; attribution after the first line only.

## Favorite constructions

- Lists of supplies as a way to show state of mind.
- A device that ends a paragraph: a sound that is not the thing hoped for.

## Register

- Plain, concrete, unadorned; technical nouns (насос, дизель, свая) carry the mood.

## Never uses

- Named emotions; rhetorical questions in narration; exclamation marks.
