# June Okafor

## Summary

Deckhand on the island ferry for six seasons; counts everything twice, trusts the count over people.

## True now

On the last run of the season, ticket book in her jacket, a cut on her left thumb from a frayed line.

## History

Grew up on the island, left for the mainland at eighteen, came back at twenty-five without saying why.

## Appearance

Tall, rain jacket two sizes big, hair tied with a bootlace; a cut on the left thumb wrapped in electrical tape; wears the ticket punch on a cord around her neck.

## Voice

Short questions, numbers, the names of things. "Ten tickets. Eleven people." Never says "I think"; says "count again."

## Wants, needs, obstacle

Wants the crossing to go by the book. Needs to admit she came back to the island because nobody else would. The old woman is the obstacle: she does not fit the count.

## Relationships

Captain Halloran: respect, no warmth. Her mother on the island: calls on Sundays, says little.

## Secrets

June has a ticket for the mainland in her own name, bought last week, unused.

## Sources

Original character.
