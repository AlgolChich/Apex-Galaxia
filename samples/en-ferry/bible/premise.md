# Premise

## Premise

On the night the island ferry makes its last run of the season, the deckhand finds a passenger who bought a ticket for a crossing that was never scheduled.

## Synopsis

Autumn, the final ferry before the strait freezes. Deckhand June Okafor counts eleven passengers against ten tickets. The eleventh is an old woman with a cardboard suitcase and a ticket dated for tomorrow. The captain wants her off the boat; June wants to know how she got on. By the time the lights of the mainland show, June has found out what the woman is carrying, and the ferry is no longer going where the timetable says.

## Genre and tone

Quiet coastal drama with a thread of mystery. Diesel, wet rope, strong tea, people who say less than they know.

## Theme

What you owe the place that raised you. Nobody says it.
