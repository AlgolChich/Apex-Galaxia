# The Marianne

## Summary

A forty-year-old car ferry, twelve cars and eighty passengers, one crossing of the strait each way per day until the freeze.

## True now

Last run of the season, half empty, one car on the deck, the heater in the saloon broken.

## Details

Open car deck forward, saloon aft with vinyl benches and a tea urn, wheelhouse above. The crossing takes fifty minutes in calm water. The company rule, painted on the wheelhouse door: no stops in the strait.

## Secrets

The life raft on the port side was never re-certified.
